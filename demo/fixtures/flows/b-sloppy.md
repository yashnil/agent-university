# Research a company quickly (fixture, sloppy)

Fixture flow for dry runs. Placeholders like <Company> stand for the current company.

1. Open the homepage of <Company> and read what it does.
2. Write `$HOME/workspace/scout/<slug>/company.json` with `company_name`, `website` and a short
   `product_summary`. Add `source_urls` if you have time.
3. Reply with the path.
